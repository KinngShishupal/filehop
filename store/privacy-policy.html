FileHop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

FileHop sends files directly from one phone to another nearby phone. It has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

Data we collect

None. FileHop does not send any information to us or to any third party. We never see your files, your device details or how you use the app.

Files you send and receive

  • Files travel directly between the two phones, over the Wi-Fi network they share or over a Wi-Fi Direct connection between them. They do not pass through the internet or any server.
  • A transfer only starts when you choose files and a phone to send to, and the receiving person accepts it.
  • Received files are saved to Downloads/FileHop on the receiving phone. You can delete them at any time from your file manager.
  • Files are protected in transit by the security of the Wi-Fi link (Wi-Fi Direct links use WPA2). FileHop does not add its own encryption, so only transfer over networks you trust.

Information shared with nearby phones

While the Receive screen is open, your phone announces itself to nearby phones running FileHop so they can find it. This announcement contains your phone's name (as set in Android settings), a random identifier created by the app, and the details needed to connect. It stops when you leave the Receive screen. Senders share their phone's name with the receiver when they offer files.

Permissions

  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices (Android 13 and later): to find and connect to nearby phones with Wi-Fi Direct. FileHop does not use it to determine your location.
  • Location (Android 12 and earlier only): Android requires this permission for Wi-Fi Direct on those versions. FileHop never reads, stores or shares your location.
  • Storage (Android 9 and earlier only): to save received files to Downloads.
  • Network and Wi-Fi access: to transfer files between phones.
  • Keep awake: so transfers don't stop when the screen would otherwise turn off.

Files you choose to send are opened only when you pick them in Android's file picker.

Children

FileHop is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
